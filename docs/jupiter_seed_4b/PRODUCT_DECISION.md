# Jupiter Seed 4B: Product Decision

**Verdict: APPROVED for Foundation Sprint**

Jupiter Seed 4B is approved as AgenThink’s first commercial model initiative. This represents the first practical stage of the Jupiter roadmap, which progresses from Seed 4B to a 30B MoE, 70B, 200B, 500B, and eventually the 20T north-star target.

## Product Objective

**Design:** Jupiter Seed 4B

**Positioning:**
An Arabic-English, GCC-specialized enterprise language model designed for private deployment, quantization, and orchestration through AgenThink Mesh.

**Initial Domains:**
1. GCC banking
2. Islamic finance
3. Telecom operations
4. Government and regulation
5. Energy and petrochemicals
6. Logistics and supply-chain resilience
7. Enterprise decision analysis

**Scope Limitation:**
This is *not* a general-purpose frontier-model project. The objective is to outperform comparable general models on a defined GCC enterprise benchmark while remaining small enough for economical private deployment.

## Open-Model Strategy

The strategy utilizes a three-layer open-weight policy. Note: Jupiter Seed will not be called "open source" unless the code, weights, and applicable materials satisfy an established open-source definition; "open-weight" is the appropriate term.

### 1. Open Layer
- Jupiter Seed core weights
- Inference code
- Model card
- Benchmark methodology
- Quantization recipes
- Reproducible evaluation scripts

### 2. Controlled Commercial Layer
- Proprietary customer fine-tunes
- Regulated-sector expert packs
- Customer-specific adapters
- Private data connectors
- Deployment configurations

### 3. AgenThink Proprietary Layer
- AgenThink Mesh
- MeshPilot optimization
- Enterprise governance
- Routing policies
- Outcome Ledger
- Decision Twin integrations
- Managed deployment
- Monitoring and support
